# Review checklist

1. Identify the apparent document or image type without claiming hidden metadata.
2. Extract the central purpose and the most important visible facts.
3. Check visible names, dates, amounts, identifiers, obligations, warnings, and exceptions when present.
4. Distinguish explicit content from interpretation.
5. Record material unreadable, cropped, contradictory, or missing content as unverified.
6. Keep observations ordered by importance and avoid duplicate findings.
